Getting There

Doheny State Beach in Dana Point, about a mile from the I-5

  1. 1

    Arrive at the park

    Doheny State Beach is at 25300 Dana Point Harbor Drive, just off I-5 at Del Obispo / Dana Point Harbor Dr. The park is open 6 AM to 10 PM daily.

  2. 2

    Pay and park in the North Day-Use lot

    Pay the day-use fee via the QR code or automated pay station, then park in the North Day-Use lot.

  3. 3

    Walk to Picnic Area 3

    We will be on the lawn at Picnic Area 3. Open the picnic area map to see exactly where.

Parking

A few things that will make the morning easier

Parking fee

About $15–$20 per car. Please bring a credit or debit card. American Express is not accepted.

Carpool

Weekend parking can fill up. If you can, ride together so there are fewer cars to park.

Arrive early

Give yourself time to park and walk over to the lawn before we begin at 11 AM.

Vehicles can’t drive onto sidewalks or into the picnic areas to unload food or supplies. Please use the parking stalls.

What to Bring

We will be outside, so please be ready for the weather

Sun protection

A hat, sunglasses and sunscreen.

Layers

It can be breezy near the ocean, so bring a sweater or jacket.

Comfortable shoes

We’ll be on grass. A chair or blanket is welcome if you’d like one.

Water

Bring a bottle to stay refreshed.

Please check the forecast before you head out.

Good to Know

Shade

Picnic Areas 1–4 have shade structures.

Dogs

Leashed dogs are welcome in the picnic and day-use areas, but not on the sand or in the water.

Alcohol

Allowed in the picnic areas only with an approved waiver or permit from park Special Events.
